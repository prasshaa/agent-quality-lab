# Product thesis

Agent Quality Lab is a **simulation-to-release quality system for autonomous AI agents**.

It helps AI teams determine whether an agent is safe, reliable and effective enough to deploy by:

1. testing realistic scenarios,
2. capturing the full trajectory,
3. verifying real outcomes,
4. diagnosing failures,
5. converting meaningful failures into regression tests,
6. recommending SHIP, CANARY or BLOCK.

LLM-as-a-judge is one evaluator inside the Lab, not the Lab itself.
